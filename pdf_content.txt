■■ Project Overview & Architecture
LegalEase AI is an empathetic, knowledge-intensive AI assistant built on a Retrieval-Augmented Generation
(RAG) architecture. It ingests authoritative Indian legal corpora — BNS, BNSS, BSA, IT Act 2000, Consumer
Protection Act 2019, POSH Act 2013, and Model Tenancy Act 2021 — and delivers plain-language legal
awareness to Indian citizens across socioeconomic strata.
High-Level Architecture
Layer
Component
Responsibility
Frontend
React / Flutter Web
Chat UI, voice input, multilingual toggle
API Gateway
FastAPI / Node.js
Session mgmt, rate limiting, auth
AI Core
LLM + RAG Pipeline
Intent detection, context retrieval, response synthesis
Knowledge Base
Vector DB (Pinecone / Weaviate)
Chunked legal corpus with metadata
Safety Layer
Rule Engine + Classifiers
Guardrails, disclaimer injection, emergency routing
Integrations
Govt APIs / SMS / Email
Complaint portals, helpline triggers
Core Design Principles
 Accuracy-first: All responses grounded in cited legal sections — no hallucinations.
 Empathy-by-design: Tone calibrated to reduce user anxiety and increase clarity.
 Safety-gated: Every legal pathway response appended with a mandatory disclaimer.
 Scalability: Stateless API design; horizontal scaling for concurrent user load.
 Accessibility: Multilingual support (Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil, Bengali, etc.) via translation layer.

■ Module 1 — Legal Rights Awareness
M
1
Legal Rights Awareness Engine
■ Scope
 Consumer Rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019
 Women's Statutory Rights (maternity, protection from harassment)
 Labour & Workplace Rights (Wages Code, Industrial Disputes Act)
 Property & Inheritance Rights (Transfer of Property Act, Hindu Succession Act)
 Senior Citizen Welfare (Maintenance & Welfare of Parents Act, 2007)
■■ Functional Requirements & Actions
Step 1. Intent Classification: NLP classifier detects the legal theme from free-text input (e.g., 'My employer
hasn't paid me' → Labour Rights).
Step 2. Act Mapping: RAG pipeline retrieves relevant act, chapter, and section from the knowledge base (e.g.,
Code on Wages, 2019 — Section 17 for delayed payment).
Step 3. Rights Explanation: LLM synthesizes a jargon-free explanation of the user's statutory rights, obligations
of the opposite party, and the remedy available.
Step 4. Structured Output: Response is formatted with: Rights Summary | Governing Law | Steps to Enforce |
Escalation Path.
Step 5. Disclaimer Injection: Mandatory disclaimer appended to every response by the safety middleware.
■ Suggested Tech Stack
Python
LangChain
Pinecone
OpenAI / Claude API
FastAPI
React
■ APIs / Integrations
 Ministry of Labour & Employment — Labour Law portal for wage grievances
 National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC) — edaakhil.nic.in API
 National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) — complaint status webhook
■ Acceptance Criteria
 System correctly identifies legal theme with ≥ 90% accuracy on a 200-query benchmark.
 Every response cites at least one specific section of the applicable Indian act.
 Response time ≤ 4 seconds for 95th percentile of production traffic.
 Disclaimer present in 100% of rights-explanation responses (automated test).

Cybercrime Guidance Engine
■ Scope
 Online Financial Fraud (UPI fraud, phishing, fake investment schemes)
 Identity Theft & Account Takeover
 Cyberstalking & Online Harassment
 Ransomware, Malware & Data Extortion
 Cyberbullying (especially targeting minors)
■■ Functional Requirements & Actions
Step 1. Crime Categorization: Multi-label classifier identifies cybercrime sub-type from user's narrative (supports
overlapping categories e.g., 'phishing AND financial fraud').
Step 2. Legal Section Mapping: Maps offense to IT Act, 2000 sections (e.g., Sec. 66C — Identity Theft, Sec.
66D — Cheating by personation) and BNS equivalents.
Step 3. Complaint Walkthrough: Generates a chronological, numbered guide for filing on cybercrime.gov.in —
account creation, report category, evidence upload, acknowledgment.
Step 4. Evidence Checklist: Dynamically generates a context-specific evidence list (bank transaction IDs, wallet
screenshots, sender metadata, URL with timestamp).
Step 5. 1930 Helpline Trigger: If financial fraud detected AND amount > threshold configured, surface the
National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930) as the first priority action.
Step 6. Follow-Up Tracking: Generate a reference ID checklist template for the user to track their complaint
status on the portal.

Women's Safety & Emergency Legal Response
■ Scope
 Domestic Violence — Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005
 Workplace Sexual Harassment — POSH Act, 2013 (Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act)
 Cyberstalking targeting women — IT Act Sec. 66A / BNS provisions
 Emergency Protection Orders & Shelter access
 NCW Complaint Filing & District Protection Officer access
■■ Functional Requirements & Actions
Step 1. Danger Signal Detection: Sentiment & keyword classifier flags physical-danger indicators ('hitting me',
'afraid to go home', 'threatening') and routes to EMERGENCY MODE.
Step 2. Emergency Header: In EMERGENCY MODE, response begins with verified helpline numbers (112,
1091, 181) BEFORE any legal information — non-negotiable UX rule.
Step 3. Zero FIR Guidance: Explains BNSS provision for Zero FIR — any police station must register,
regardless of jurisdiction — with step-by-step filing instructions.
Step 4. Protection Officer Locator: Integrates district Protection Officer directory (Ministry of Women & Child
Development data) to surface nearest officer.
Step 5. POSH Complaint Flow: For workplace harassment, explains ICC (Internal Complaints Committee)
obligation, 90-day filing window, and escalation to LCC if no ICC exists.
Step 6. NCW Filing Assistance: Guides user through ncwapps.nic.in online complaint form with required
document checklist.
Step 7. Safe Language Protocol: Responses avoid re-traumatizing language; use trauma-informed
communication patterns reviewed by licensed counsellors.

Tenant-Landlord Disputes & Business Compliance
■ Scope
 Rental Disputes — Model Tenancy Act 2021 & state-specific Rent Control Acts
 Security Deposit Rules — cap limits, return timelines, deduction rights
 Eviction Procedures — notice periods, grounds, and due process
 Startup Compliance Checklist — GST registration, Trademark, Company incorporation basics
 Consumer rights for homebuyers under RERA (Real Estate Regulation Act)
■■ Functional Requirements & Actions
Step 1. State Detection: Infer or ask user's state to apply correct Rent Control Act (e.g., Maharashtra Rent
Control Act vs Delhi Rent Act vs MTA 2021 defaults).
Step 2. Issue Classification: Identify dispute type — deposit withholding, illegal eviction, rent increase dispute,
maintenance non-compliance — and surface specific provisions.
Step 3. Security Deposit Calculator: Interactive module — input monthly rent → output permissible deposit cap
under applicable state law + interest entitlement formula.
Step 4. Notice Period Guide: Explain mandatory notice periods for both landlord and tenant across common
scenarios (non-payment, lease expiry, sale of property).
Step 5. Eviction Rights Explainer: Clarify grounds under which landlord CAN and CANNOT evict; explain
self-help eviction prohibition and legal remedy via Rent Authority.
Step 6. Business Compliance Checklist: Generate role-specific compliance checklist (Sole Proprietor / LLP / Pvt
Ltd) covering GST, PAN, TAN, Trademark, Shops Act.
Step 7. RERA Complaint Path: For homebuyer disputes, guide to state RERA portal complaint registration with
required documents list.

■ Module 5 — Legal Document Simplifier
M
5
Legal Document Simplifier & Risk Analyzer
■ Scope
 Rental Agreements — lease deed analysis, clause-by-clause breakdown
 Employment Contracts — probation terms, notice pay, IP assignment clauses
 Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) — scope, duration, carve-outs, penalties
 Service Agreements & SaaS Terms — liability caps, SLA, data ownership
 Basic Affidavits — format validation and content explanation
■■ Functional Requirements & Actions
Step 1. Document Ingestion: Accept PDF / DOCX / pasted text. Parse and chunk document into logical clause
segments using structural NLP (heading detection + boundary rules).
Step 2. Clause Classification: Tag each clause by type — Payment, Termination, Penalty, Confidentiality,
Jurisdiction, IP Assignment, Indemnity, Limitation of Liability.
Step 3. Plain-English Translation: For each clause, generate a ≤ 3-sentence plain summary explaining 'what this
means for YOU' in non-legal language.
Step 4. Obligation Extraction: Structured output of WHO owes WHAT by WHEN — two columns: 'Your
Obligations' and 'Their Obligations'.
Step 5. Risk Flag Engine: Automated detection of high-risk patterns — extreme penalty clauses (> 5x contract
value), unilateral termination without notice, perpetual IP assignment, jurisdiction in distant/inconvenient court,
auto-renewal without notification.
Step 6. Risk Scoring Dashboard: Visual risk summary — Green (standard) / Amber (review) / Red (negotiate or
reject) — for each clause category.
Step 7. Negotiation Tips: For each Red-flagged clause, suggest a standard market-norm counter-clause
language the user can propose.

■■ Cross-Cutting Module — Guardrails & Safety
All 5 feature modules share a centralized Safety & Guardrail Middleware layer. This layer is non-bypassable and
runs as a post-processing step on every LLM response before it is returned to the user.
Guardrail
Trigger Condition
Action
Mandatory Disclaimer
Any response containing legal pathways, act sections, or procedure guides
Append standardized disclaimer text — 100% coverage enforced by automated test
No Case Outcome Prediction
LLM output contains phrases like 'you will win', 'guaranteed refund', 'court will decide in your favor'
Block response, regenerate with instruction: 'Focus on process, not outcome'
Emergency Interception
Danger keywords detected (domestic violence escalation, active extortion, suicidal ideation)
Prepend verified helpline block (112, 1930, 1091, iCall) before all other content
Hallucination Guard
LLM cites a section not found in the RAG-retrieved context
Flag for review; replace with: 'Please consult a legal professional for this specific query'
PII Redaction
Response accidentally echoes user-provided PAN, Aadhaar, phone, or bank numbers
Regex + NER scrubber masks sensitive data before delivery
