      Zero FIR Guide (Section 173 BNSS)

      Under the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), a victim of crime can file an FIR at any police station, regardless of the crime location.

      1

      Go to the nearest police station (regardless of jurisdiction).

      2

      State that you wish to file a Zero FIR.

      3

      The police must write the complaint and assign a serial number starting with '0'.

      4

      The station will transfer the case to the appropriate jurisdiction for active investigation.

      POSH Act, 2013 (Workplace Harassment)

      Legal safeguards and compliance schedule for sexual harassment complaints at workplaces:

      Requirement Mandated Timeline / Detail
      ICC Constitution Mandatory for every office/organization with 10+ employees.
      Filing Window Complaint must be filed in writing within 3 months of the incident.
      Inquiry Period Must be completed by the ICC within a strict window of 90 days.
      Action / Appeal Action must be taken within 60 days of report. Appeal allowed within 90 days.
      File online on Central Ministry Portal: SHe-Box Portal
